# Community guidelines

Keep issues, reviews and discussions focused on the project. Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique the code or behavior rather than attacking a person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Use fictional examples and respect other people's licenses and attribution.

Report conduct concerns privately to [ai@widechaos.cn](mailto:ai@widechaos.cn). The maintainer may remove abusive content or restrict participation to keep the project usable.
