# Callout Gallery

> [!note] Note
> General context and supporting information.

> [!abstract] Abstract
> Summary, overview, or TL;DR.

> [!info] Info
> Neutral facts and reference material.

> [!todo] Todo
> A next action or unfinished item.

> [!tip] Tip
> A helpful shortcut or better approach.

> [!success] Success
> A verified or completed outcome.

> [!question] Question
> Something that still needs an answer.

> [!warning] Warning
> Check the configuration before continuing.

> [!caution] Caution
> Proceed carefully.

> [!failure] Failure
> A failed or missing requirement.

> [!danger] Danger
> A high-risk or destructive operation.

> [!bug] Bug
> A confirmed defect or reproducible problem.

> [!example] Example
> A sample input, output, or implementation.

> [!quote] Quote
> Good hierarchy shows importance before completeness.
